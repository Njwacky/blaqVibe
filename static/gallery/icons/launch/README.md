# Launch icon sources

These SVGs are stored locally so the Launch guide does not depend on third-party CDNs. They identify publishing destinations only; all brand names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners.

Retrieved 14 August 2026:

- `brands/cloudflare.svg`, `vercel.svg`, `render.svg`, `docker.svg`, `google-play.svg`, `app-store.svg`, `apple.svg`, `steam.svg`, and `windows.svg`: [SVGL](https://github.com/pheralb/svgl) at `a5a7fdef6454504902c33f571517e8302af3448c` (MIT).
- `brands/itchio.svg`, `pwa.svg`, `flathub.svg`, and `android.svg`: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) at `34c22501f9ac9f22b12f825677ccbab1fb22e14b` (CC0 collection; individual trademark/licence guidance still applies).
- `brands/chrome-web-store.svg`: [SVG Logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos) at `4de741f8503d5e81abf5dfa05214690e938296bf` (CC0).
- `brands/microsoft-store.svg`: the Simple Icons Microsoft Store glyph via [Blade Simple Icons](https://github.com/ublabs/blade-simple-icons) (Simple Icons artwork is distributed under CC0; Microsoft trademarks remain Microsoft's).
- `artifacts/*.svg`: original interface symbols created for BlaqVibes.

The monochrome Simple Icons assets have only been assigned their documented brand colour (or a high-contrast white treatment); their paths are unchanged.
