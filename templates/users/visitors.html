How “unique” is counted: one HMAC of IP + browser per install, with no cookie and no JavaScript. A shared office network can look like one visitor, and a browser update can make one person look like two — the number is an honest estimate, not a fingerprint. How time is counted: server-side, from the timestamps of page views. “On page” is the gap until their next page view; “on site” is the run of pages with no gap longer than 30 minutes. A tab left open and walked away from cannot be told apart from reading — there is no heartbeat script — so treat long times as an upper bound. Not tracked: static files, redirects, JSON/API calls, 404s, the admin tools, and anyone the site never served a page to.
