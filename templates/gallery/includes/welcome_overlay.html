{% comment %} Action-first first-time welcome. 5 Whys: 1. Why change the old four-screen intro? New builders need to do something before they understand a platform; a presentation delays the first useful action. 2. Why make the first action an upload? BlaqVibes' core promise is that a build becomes visible proof. The fastest way to demonstrate that promise is to let the person put one real build into the product. 3. Why keep the upload lightweight? Requiring the full proof form before the first reward makes a new visitor feel like they are filling in paperwork. 4. Why keep safety scanning? Removing friction must not remove the trust boundary. We skip optional questions, never the security pipeline. 5. Why offer Explore as the alternative? Some people need to see the community before committing a project; exploration should be a valid first action, not a dead-end. The modal is still account-scoped by Profile.overlay_seen. "Upload my first app" hands off to /publish/?welcome=1, where the server records that the intro was answered before rendering the upload form. {% endcomment %}
