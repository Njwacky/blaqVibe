{% comment %} One attention case, rendered identically on the centre page and on its own page — one markup, one set of buttons, so a decision can never behave differently depending on where the owner happened to click it. Expects: `case` (with `case.ranked` and `case.countdown` attached by the view). The facts about each copy come from AttentionCandidate.snapshot, written from server-side columns when the case opens. That is what lets this block keep saying "uploaded 02 Jan 2026, 14 files, never published" about a copy that has already been deleted — the decision has to stay readable after the cleanup, or the owner cannot tell what they just agreed to. Progressive enhancement: "Keep my pick" is NOT disabled here. attention.js disables it until a copy is chosen; without JS the button still posts, the browser's own `required` catches an empty pick, and the engine refuses politely if both of those are bypassed. A deadline nobody can click is worse than a deadline that answers "pick which copy to keep". {% endcomment %}
