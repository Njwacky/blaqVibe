THE CORE IDEA

Before you add words,
ask why five times.

Most weak prompts solve the surface request. The 5 Whys method finds the decision or outcome underneath it—so you can ask for the useful thing once.

01What do you need?State the deliverable, not the topic.
02Why do you need it?Find the decision it should support.
03Who will use it?Set the audience and their level.
04What must be true?Add facts, limits, and evidence.
05How will you check it?Define a visible success test.
