What we check — and what we honestly don't
Virus scanClamAV scans ZIPs before publishing.
Secrets scanChecks ZIPs and snippets for API keys, tokens, and private keys. Filenames may be reviewed; secret values stay in the scan.
Dependency auditnpm audit / pip-audit check manifests; registry checks catch nonexistent package names.
What we don't doApps aren't executed; previews use a locked-down sandbox iframe. Unsupported projects say “no live preview.” Badges confirm scans, not behavior.
Reviews are humanStars and reviews are human feedback; badges are scan evidence.
How fakes are handled
Badges can't be bought or edited. Only the scan pipeline assigns them.

Changes reset the badge. Re-scans are required after edits; buyers receive the checked files.

Pending or quarantined projects have no badge. If scanning is offline, auto-publishing waits.

Found a problem? Report it on the project page. Confirmed reports can remove it from the feed.
